## Overview

**Apple Enchant** allows you to craft Enchanted Golden Apples without breaking vanilla balance. By sacrificing your hard-earned experience levels, you can imbue standard Golden Apples with powerful magic.

🔗 **Synergy:** Highly recommended to use alongside **[Food Balance](https://modrinth.com/datapack/food-balance)** and **[Mooshroom Energy](https://modrinth.com/datapack/energy)** for expanded food mechanics!

---

## How to Enchant

Transforming your golden apples requires magical mastery and raw experience:

- **Requirement:** You must have **at least 30 experience levels**.
- **Action:** Stand on an **Enchanting Table** and **crouch (sneak)** while holding Golden Apples in your inventory.
- **Cost:** The ritual consumes exactly **1395 XP** (the exact total amount needed to go from level 0 to 30).
- **VFX/SFX:** Enjoy mysterious custom sound and particle effects during the enchanting process!

> Do not begin the enchanting ritual if you aren't absolutely ready to spend all 30 levels!

## Disenchanting & Recovering XP

Need your experience back? You can reverse the magical process:

- **Cook** an Enchanted Golden Apple in a standard **Furnace** to recover the spent XP.
- **Do NOT use a Campfire!** Campfires will cook the item without returning any experience.

### The Shattered Golden Apple

When you successfully disenchant an apple in a furnace, you are left with a new, depleted item: the **Shattered Golden Apple**.

- **Effect:** Grants **Decaying Resistance (🛡️ 100% → 0%)**.
- **Drawback:** Provides **zero healing** to the player.
